    Inside the brain (live)

      This is a small rate-based network, not the real connectome — 139,255 neurons and 54.5 million synapses (FlyWire Consortium, Nature, 2024) can't run in a browser tab. Region layout and pathways follow real fly neuroanatomy; connection weights are illustrative, tuned so competing drives resolve the way documented circuits behave qualitatively (e.g. the Giant Fiber escape reflex overriding other behavior, or persistent threat arousal outlasting the threat itself) — not measured values from the dataset.
