A female fly brain, deciding

Internal state

Leaky integrate and fire neurons, 1 ms steps, run in a background thread capped at about three quarters of one CPU core; it pauses when this tab is hidden. Cell counts follow FlyWire (Dorkenwald et al., Nature 2024); the wiring is statistical, random partners within each known pathway, not the measured 54.5 million synapses. Behaviour comes from descending and motor neurons: Giant Fiber (escape), freeze DNs, steering DNs, vpoDN (receptivity) and proboscis motor neurons (feeding). Mushroom body synapses learn from dopamine.
